How it behaves

  • Speech is scripted. Words, fillers, pauses and off-topic moments play on a timer. You grade yourself.
  • Community Talk: other people's answers are scripted stand-ins. Audio is simulated.
  • Camera is a placeholder. Face thumbnails show as rating circles.
  • The four days before today are sample data. Your recordings and saved cards stay in this browser.
  • Cards: swipe, or use Back, Next and the arrow keys. Dive Deeper stays on one topic and moves the gradient to the background.
